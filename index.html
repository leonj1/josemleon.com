About Jose Explorer

Jose Explorer

A personal website inspired by the classic Process Explorer interface from Sysinternals.

The process list becomes a site map. The lower pane becomes a page reader. Familiar menus, colored rows, and a movable pane divider keep the spirit of the original.

Independent homage by Jose M. Leon. No affiliation with Microsoft or Sysinternals.
